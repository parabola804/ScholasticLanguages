Picking a voice plays a short sample. The choice is remembered on this device only, so each device can have its own. If a voice sounds wrong or stays silent, try another.

📋 Jump to a section

    Tap 🔊 to hear a phrase spoken aloud. Tap "▶ Play all" on a section to hear every phrase in it, first in Spanish and then in English — tap it again to stop. "▶ Play all sections" at the top does the same for the whole list, start to finish, with a stop button that stays on screen. If a voice doesn't sound right on this device, tap "Voices" to choose a different one. The "Sounds like" button shows or hides the pronunciation guide. To add new words from the teacher, the phrase list in the file gets updated and redeployed — every iPad picks up the new list automatically next time it's opened. You can also tap "Edit words" to make quick local changes on this iPad directly.
